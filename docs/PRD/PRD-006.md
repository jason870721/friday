---

## 1. Summary

Move trade-direction discovery out of the LLM and into a **deterministic Go
strategy engine**. A registry of pluggable strategies computes objective,
backtestable signals from candles+indicators; an aggregator combines them
into a consensus. The Analyst's job changes from *inventing* a direction to
*validating* the consensus against macro/sentiment context — it can veto
with a cited reason, but it can't go rogue.

---

## 2. Problem

The setup-trigger logic currently lives in the Analyst prompt
(`analystSystemPrompt`, formerly `prompt.go`'s "Setup Triggers"). An LLM
pattern-matching triggers is **inconsistent** (non-deterministic sampling),
**untestable**, has **no statistical edge**, and is **overfit to prompt
wording** — every prompt tweak changes behaviour unpredictably with no
isolated strategy to improve.

---

## 3. Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals**
- A pluggable `internal/strategy` package producing typed `Signal`s.
- A consensus aggregator surfaced to the Analyst as context.
- Re-cast the Analyst as a signal *validator* (approve / veto-with-reason).

**Non-Goals**
- Not claiming the heuristic strategies beat discretion — the value is
  traceability + backtestability; strategies are iterable.
- No change to Risk Manager sizing or Executor mechanics.

---

## 4. Requirements

### 4.1 `internal/strategy/` (new)

- **R1.** Core types: `Signal{Symbol, Direction, Confidence, Reason,
  Invalidation, Strategy}`, `Direction (Neutral/Long/Short)`, and the
  `Strategy` interface (`Name()`, `Analyze(symbol, candles) Signal`).
- **R2.** `Registry` (`AnalyzeAll`) and `Aggregator` (`Aggregate(signals)
  Consensus`) — majority-vote with confidence weighting; mixed → Neutral.
- **R3.** Initial strategies as Go ports of the existing triggers:
  `momentum.go`, `breakout.go`, `mean_revert.go`, `divergence.go`. Reuse
  `binance.SMA/RSI`; add `ADX` to `indicators.go` for regime/confidence.
- **R4.** `strategy_test.go` — fixture-based tests with known candle
  patterns per strategy and for the aggregator's consensus rules.

### 4.2 Wiring (current architecture)

- **R5.** Compute consensus from the candles the Analyst already pulls and
  surface it where the Analyst reads — extend the `binance_klines` Summary
  via a `SemanticSummaryWithSignals(ks, consensus)` in
  [`internal/binance/indicators.go`](../../internal/binance/indicators.go),
  or attach a `strategy_signals` field to the Analyst's data. Cross-symbol
  **divergence** needs all symbols at once → two-pass (collect per-symbol
  signals, then run divergence) or compute it in the orchestrator before
  the Analyst runs; document the chosen approach.
- **R6.** Re-cast `analystSystemPrompt` in
  [`internal/orchestrator/prompts.go`](../../internal/orchestrator/prompts.go):
  the Analyst's per-symbol `bias` must agree with the consensus *or* cite a
  specific overriding data point (e.g. "Fear & Greed 85 extreme greed
  overrides the momentum-long"). It may not assert a direction that
  contradicts all signals without such a citation. (The Risk Manager
  retains its independent veto from PRD-003.)

---

## 5. Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Each strategy returns the expected `Signal` on its fixture; the
      aggregator yields Long/Short/Neutral per the documented rules. —
      `internal/strategy/strategy_test.go` (momentum/breakout/mean-reversion/
      divergence + aggregator rules).
- [x] The strategy consensus appears in the Analyst's input (klines Summary
      or equivalent) every round. — `binance_klines` appends
      `strategy.FormatSummary(...)` via `strategy.ConsensusFor`.
- [x] In a live round, the Analyst's bias matches the consensus or carries
      an explicit cited override; it never silently contradicts all signals.
      — `analystSystemPrompt` re-cast as validator (anchor + cited override).
      Live LLM compliance to confirm in a runtime session.
- [x] `go build ./...` and `go test ./...` pass.

### Decisions & notes

- **Analyst constraint = anchor + cited override** (chosen): bias defaults
  to the consensus direction; the Analyst may flip to NEUTRAL only by citing
  a specific data point, and may not contradict a directional consensus
  without one.
- **Strategies:** `momentum`, `breakout`, `mean_reversion` are single-symbol
  and wired live; `ADX(14)` added to `indicators.go` and boosts
  momentum/breakout confidence when a strong trend is present (degrades
  gracefully when the candle window is too short for ADX).
- **Divergence is implemented and unit-tested** (`DivergenceSignals`) but
  **not yet wired live** — the per-symbol `binance_klines` path sees one
  symbol at a time. Live wiring needs a cross-symbol pass and pairs
  naturally with PRD-008's multi-symbol `binance_mtf_klines`.

---

## 6. Out of Scope / Follow-ups

- Backtest-driven auto-tuning of strategy parameters (uses PRD-004's
  `run_backtest`).
- A "WAIT-override" channel for the LLM to flag opportunities no strategy
  covers, logged for future strategy development.
