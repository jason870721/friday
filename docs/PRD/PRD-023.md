## 1. Summary

Three prompt-level changes to the Analyst that improve decision quality without
touching the strategy engine:

1. **Regime-aware bias clamp** — prevents counter-trend LONGs in a bearish trending
   market (8 LONGs lost -$176.48 in a declining BTC from ~74000 to ~71100).

2. **Fee-aware entry rule + fee budget surface** — makes the Analyst conscious of
   commission costs (45.4% of gross losses) and surfaces the existing
   `FeeBudget` status into the round prompt (PRD-020 R9 was never wired).

3. **Recall memory minimum-sample threshold** — breaks the negative feedback loop
   where `recall_trades` returning "3筆全虧" on 2-3 trades blocks all new entries,
   preventing the system from ever accumulating wins.

---

## 2. Problem

### 2.1 Counter-trend LONGs driven by Fear & Greed

Fear & Greed at 23–29 repeatedly triggered "逆勢做多" logic (entry reasons:
"極度恐慌(23)經典逆勢做多", "恐懼28順風"). `strategy.DetectRegime` (PRD-016)
already classifies the market as TRENDING/RANGING/TRANSITIONAL, but the Analyst
prompt has no rule restricting LONG bias in a bearish trend. All 8 LONG trades
lost money, accounting for -$176.48 of the -$198.29 total loss.

### 2.2 Fee blindness

Commissions consumed -$61.95 (45.4% of gross losses). PRD-020 implemented
`FeeBudget` (30min rolling window, 0.5%-of-balance cap), but:
- The Analyst prompt never mentions fees.
- PRD-020 R9 ("round prompt includes fee-budget status when nearing the limit")
  was never wired into the orchestrator's prompt builder.
- Trades with expected moves smaller than 3× round-trip fee were opened.

### 2.3 Recall negative feedback loop

`recall_trades` (PRD-004) searches for similar historical trades and reports
win/loss. With only 2-3 similar trades (all losses given the 8.3% overall win
rate), the Analyst cites "回溯相似條件交易全部虧損" to veto entries. This
creates a self-reinforcing cycle: losses → recall shows all losses → no new
trades → no chance to accumulate wins → recall continues showing all losses.

---

## 3. Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals**
- Stop the Analyst from opening LONGs in a bearish trend without MTF confirmation.
- Make the Analyst aware of fee impact and the fee budget status.
- Prevent low-sample recall data from being treated as statistically meaningful.

**Non-Goals**
- No change to the strategy engine or signal generation.
- No change to how `FeeBudget` enforces its hard limit (PRD-020 already does this).
- No time-decay weighting for recall (deferred follow-up).

---

## 4. Requirements

### 4.1 Regime-aware bias clamp — `internal/orchestrator/prompts.go`

- **R1.** Add to `analystSystemPrompt` (after the MTF/Cross-TF section ~line 134):

  > **Regime-aware bias rule:** When the `Regime:` line shows **TRENDING** and
  > the 4h price is below 4h MA20 (bearish trend):
  > - LONG bias requires BOTH: (a) `MTF Strategy:` explicitly says LONG, AND
  >   (b) Fear & Greed ≤ 25 (extreme fear). Without both, you MUST use NEUTRAL.
  > - SHORT bias is permitted with `MTF Strategy: SHORT` or
  >   `Cross-TF: ALIGNED BEARISH`.
  > - When `Regime:` is RANGING or TRANSITIONAL, all biases are permitted as
  >   usual.

### 4.2 Fee-aware entry rule — `internal/orchestrator/prompts.go`

- **R2.** Add to `analystSystemPrompt` (near the sizing/risk section):

  > **Fee-aware sizing rule:** Every trade must expect a move that clears at
  > least **3× the round-trip taker fee** (currently ~0.08% → ~0.24% minimum
  > expected move). If the ATR(14) or strategy TP is less than 3× the fee, do
  > not open the trade. State the expected-move-to-fee ratio in your reason.

### 4.3 Fee budget surface — `internal/orchestrator/orchestrator.go`

- **R3.** In the round loop, call `FeeBudget.Status()` after each round. When
  `Status().Near` is true (≥50% of cap spent), inject a warning into the carry
  string passed to the next round's prompts:

  > `⚠️ Fee budget: $X.XX spent in the last 30m (XX% of ${threshold}% cap).`

- **R4.** `FeeBudget` is already constructed in `bootstrap.New` and wired into
  `binance_order` for hard enforcement. The orchestrator needs a reference to it
  (or the global guardrail) to read `Status()`.

### 4.4 Recall minimum sample — `internal/memory/store.go` + `internal/tool/recall_trades.go`

- **R5.** `store.Similar()` return value gains `IsConclusive bool`: `true` when
  ≥5 similar trades exist, `false` otherwise.
- **R6.** `recall_trades` tool formats inconclusive results as:
  `"insufficient data (<5 similar trades) — do not use this to veto."`
- **R7.** Add to `analystSystemPrompt` (near the `recall_trades` step):

  > When `recall_trades` returns "insufficient data", treat the result as
  > non-informative — do NOT cite it as a reason to stay NEUTRAL.

## 6. Out of Scope / Follow-ups

- Time-decay weighting for recall (older trades weighted less).
- Regime-aware position sizing (reduce size in counter-trend trades, not just block them).
- Adaptive fee threshold that tightens after consecutive losses.
- Per-symbol fee budgets.
