## 1. Summary

Size positions by **volatility**, not a flat percentage. Add ATR(14) and a
`SuggestedSize` helper that derives quantity from a fixed risk-per-trade
budget divided by an ATR-based stop distance. This gives the Risk Manager a
volatility-calibrated *target* inside the existing 15% hard cap — so BTC
(low vol) and SOL (high vol) carry comparable risk per trade.

---

## 2. Problem

A flat 15% margin cap treats BTC at ~2% daily vol the same as SOL at ~8% —
the same dollar position is ~4× the risk on SOL. Current defaults
("80–100% of max_per_pos") are volatility-blind and dangerously large.

---

## 3. Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals**
- `ATR(14)` indicator; `risk.SuggestedSize` (risk ÷ ATR-stop distance).
- Surface ATR + a sizing hint where the agents read market data.
- Risk Manager sizes from the volatility target within the 15% cap.

**Non-Goals**
- Does not replace the 15% `MarginCapValidator` cap — it's a smarter target
  beneath it.
- The actual stop *execution* is PRD-009; this PRD only computes the level.

---

  hint to the `binance_klines` Summary line (e.g. *"Sizing hint (1% risk,
  2×ATR stop): ~0.059 BTC, margin ~$205 @25x, stop ~$85,550; cap allows up
  to $1,500"*).
- **R4.** Update `riskSystemPrompt` in
  [`internal/orchestrator/prompts.go`](../../internal/orchestrator/prompts.go):
  the Risk Manager sizes OPEN/ADD from the ATR risk-based target (default
  ~1% risk-per-trade) rather than "80–100% of cap", still clamped by the
  14% target / 15% hard cap from PRD-002/003. The `StopPrice` it computes
  feeds PRD-009.

- [x] `go build ./...` and `go test ./...` pass.

### Decisions & notes

- **Quantity is leverage-independent** (risk ÷ ATR-stop); leverage only scales
  the *margin*. The klines hint uses an illustrative 20× for the margin figure
  and labels it so; the Risk Manager recomputes with its real leverage.
- **Cross-agent data flow:** ATR + the suggested stop ride in the Analyst's
  per-symbol summary (klines Summary → `submit_analysis`) to the Risk Manager,
  which holds the balance and does the dollar sizing per its prompt. The
  `binance_klines` hint fetches balance best-effort (signed) and omits the
  dollar figures if it is unavailable.
- **Cap interplay:** `SuggestedSize` clamps to `MaxMarginPct` (15%); the prompt
  clamps to the 14% target; `MarginCapValidator` is the hard backstop. The stop
  level is computed here for **PRD-009** to execute.
