## 1. Summary

A goroutine `StopMonitor` that polls price ~every second and fires
reduce-only market closes when a registered stop-loss or take-profit level
is crossed — a fast-reaction safety net that runs independently of (and
alongside) the agents' slower round cadence.

---

## 2. Problem

Stops exist only "in the LLM's mind": the agents check uPnL once per round
and may forget, get distracted across symbols, or be too slow when price
gaps. If the process is mid-round (or wedged), an open position is
effectively unprotected between cycles.

---

## 3. Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals**
- `StopMonitor` polling at ~1s that closes on SL/TP breach.
- An agent-facing tool to register/clear levels per symbol.
- A clear, documented safety net that complements (not replaces) the
  round-level risk checks.

**Non-Goals**
- Not exchange-native `STOP_MARKET` orders (heavier lifecycle, order-book
  signal leakage) — a documented follow-up; in-memory levels only.
- No persistence across process restarts (documented limitation).

---

## 4. Requirements

- **R1.** `internal/risk/stop_monitor.go`: `StopLevels{StopPrice,
  TakeProfit, PositionQty, PositionSide}`, `StopMonitor` with
  `SetLevels(symbol, levels)` (zero clears), and `Start(ctx)` polling on a
  configurable interval (1s default). Per symbol with active levels: fetch
  mark price; LONG closes on `mark ≤ stop` or `mark ≥ tp`, SHORT mirrors;
  on fire → reduce-only `MarketOrder`, log, clear that symbol's levels.
- **R2.** `stop_monitor_test.go` with a mocked client: LONG stop / SHORT
  stop / TP fire, no-trigger-within-range, mid-flight clear, duplicate
  `SetLevels` idempotence.
- **R3.** `internal/tool/binance_stop_monitor.go`: tool to register/clear
  `stop_price` + `take_profit_price` for a symbol, calling the shared
  `StopMonitor.SetLevels`. Register it for the **Executor** in
  `orchestrator.New`, and update `executorSystemPrompt` to set levels right
  after an OPEN (using PRD-007's computed `StopPrice`).
- **R4.** Start the monitor goroutine in
  [`cmd/friday/main.go`](../../cmd/friday/main.go) before `orch.Run`,
  sharing one `*binance.Client` and cancelled by the same context as the
  orchestrator loop (Ctrl+C).

---

## 5. Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Monitor fires a reduce-only close within ~1–2s of a LONG stop, a
      SHORT stop, and a TP breach; no fire while price stays in range
      (covered in `stop_monitor_test.go` with a mock client).
- [ ] `binance_stop_monitor` registers and clears levels; a cleared level
      stops triggering.
- [ ] Live: open a testnet position, set a tight stop, observe the monitor
      close it within ~2s (independent of the round cadence).
- [ ] `go build ./...` and `go test ./...` pass.

---

## 6. Out of Scope / Follow-ups

- Exchange-native `STOP_MARKET` / `TAKE_PROFIT_MARKET` as a crash-survivable
  second layer.
- Persisting active levels across restarts.
